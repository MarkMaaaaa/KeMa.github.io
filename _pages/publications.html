Recent Project

"Automated Vehicle Hardware/Algorithm Development"

Conducted from 01/2021 to Now

This project focuses on advancing hardware and algorithm design for automated vehicles (AVs). The work includes the integration of advanced sensors, real-time control units, and robust communication systems. The algorithm development emphasizes vehicle perception, decision-making, and path-following capabilities. Tests involve scenarios such as obstacle avoidance, autonomous lane changes, and adaptive cruise control, all executed on custom-built AV platforms equipped with multi-modal sensor suites. This research aims to refine AV hardware/software synergies, ensuring enhanced safety and operational efficiency in real-world applications.

"Connected Automated Vehicle: VECTOR"

Conducted from 05/2022 to Now

The VECTOR (Visual-Enhanced Cooperative Traffic Operations) project introduces a novel Vehicle-to-Vehicle Communication System designed to optimize energy consumption in connected and automated vehicles. By incorporating advanced visual communication protocols, VECTOR enables cooperative decision-making among vehicles. The research explores how real-time data sharing, such as traffic flow visualization and vehicle intentions, can reduce delays and fuel usage in dynamic traffic environments. The system is tested using both simulation platforms and field experiments, demonstrating significant improvements in cooperative traffic management.

"Eco-driving Real-world Testing"

Conducted from 03/2023 to Now

This project evaluates the effectiveness of eco-driving strategies in real-world conditions. Using a fleet of connected automated vehicles equipped with advanced sensors, the study monitors driving behaviors under varying traffic and environmental conditions. Data collected include fuel consumption, emission levels, and traffic smoothness metrics. The findings highlight how adaptive driving strategies can minimize energy usage and carbon footprint. The experiments provide valuable insights into the scalability of eco-driving technologies for large-scale deployment.
