Recent Projects

"Automated Vehicle Hardware/Algorithm Development"

Conducted from 06/2018 to Now

This project focuses on the development and validation of cutting-edge hardware and algorithms for automated vehicles. Key advancements include sensor integration, multi-modal data processing, and the design of robust control systems. Leveraging advanced AI-driven algorithms, we optimize real-time decision-making processes in various traffic and environmental scenarios. The project also entails extensive testing with customized AV platforms to ensure reliability and scalability in both urban and highway conditions.

"Connected Automated Vehicle: VECTOR"

Conducted from 01/2020 to Now

The VECTOR project aims to revolutionize Vehicle-to-Vehicle communication by integrating visual enhancements into cooperative traffic operations. This approach seeks to minimize energy consumption and improve traffic efficiency for connected and automated vehicles (CAVs). The research involves designing and testing a communication framework that incorporates multi-modal data sharing and predictive modeling for real-time adaptive driving strategies. Key results demonstrate significant reductions in energy use across diverse traffic conditions.

"Eco-Driving: Real-World Testing"

Conducted from 03/2019 to Now

Through this initiative, we explore the practical implementation of eco-driving strategies in real-world scenarios. The project involves deploying connected and automated vehicles to evaluate the impact of predictive eco-driving algorithms on fuel consumption and emissions. By combining GPS, traffic data, and dynamic vehicle models, we develop adaptive strategies for urban and highway driving. Field tests reveal substantial improvements in energy efficiency and reduced carbon footprint, supporting the feasibility of eco-driving at scale.
